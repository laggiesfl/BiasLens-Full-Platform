# Document 01 — BiasLens Division: Business Model & Operating Blueprint

**Status:** Approved foundation document  
**Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Parent:** BeAccessible / BX Accessible  
**Division:** BiasLens — AI Governance, Bias & Evidence Assurance

## Purpose

BiasLens is to operate as a distinct division rather than as a single assessment tool. Its purpose is to help organisations investigate, evidence, govern and defend the use of AI systems without reducing complex evidence to a simplistic biased/not-biased verdict.

## Commercial lines

1. **BiasLens Assess** — agent-led assessment of one AI system at a time.
2. **BiasLens Verify** — deeper evidence assurance across vendor claims, model cards, testing, procurement and governance documentation.
3. **BiasLens Monitor** — later-stage operational and telemetry evidence monitoring.
4. **BiasLens Defend** — creation and maintenance of the Algorithm Defence File and defensible governance record.
5. **BiasLens Advisory** — human-led specialist review, remediation strategy, executive briefings and complex governance support.

## Delivery tiers

| Tier | Customer receives | Human involvement |
|---|---|---|
| Agent-led | Guided BiasLens assessment | Minimal |
| Reviewed | Agent assessment plus expert validation | Moderate |
| Assured | Evidence assurance plus specialist review | High |
| Enterprise | Multi-system platform, monitoring and advisory | Strategic |

## Hybrid operating model

The agent replaces much of the manual assessment-production layer: intake, adaptive questioning, first-pass evidence review, evidence-state recording, structured documentation and draft reporting. Human specialists remain responsible for consequential judgement, complex interpretation, challenge, escalation and final expert review where required.

## Intellectual property

BiasLens IP resides in the system around the underlying AI model, including its assessment methodology, evidence taxonomy, Evidence State model, provenance framework, bias-pathway reasoning, uncertainty rules, escalation logic, control mappings, expert-review protocol, reporting structure and Algorithm Defence File architecture.

## Governance principle

BiasLens does not sell an AI that automatically decides whether another AI is biased. It provides structured evidence-based investigation with traceable findings, explicit uncertainty and human accountability.

## Revenue model

The division should support transactional and recurring revenue through assessment fees, reviewed assessments, evidence assurance, enterprise licences, monitoring, reassessment, advisory services and defence-file maintenance.

## Brand architecture

- BiasLens Assess
- BiasLens Verify
- BiasLens Monitor
- BiasLens Defend
- BiasLens Advisory

BiasLens should be managed with a separate operating view and P&L even if it initially remains legally within BeAccessible/BX Accessible.

## Initial commercial product

BiasLens 1.0 will begin with:

**BiasLens Agent Assessment + Evidence Review + Optional Expert Review + Algorithm Defence File.**

The first build focus is **BiasLens Assess — Agent-led AI System Assessment**.

## Accessibility and Universal Design

Accessibility and Universal Design are architectural requirements, not add-ons. Product workflows must target WCAG 2.2 AA as a minimum and AAA where practicable, support full keyboard operation and assistive technologies, avoid mandatory mouse or voice interaction, provide accessible evidence upload/review, preserve logical reading order, expose all states in text, support error recovery and keep consequential agent actions reviewable and reversible.
