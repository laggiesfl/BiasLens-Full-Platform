### Why BiasLens
AI systems can affect recruitment, access to services, customer experiences and organisational decisions. A dashboard alert alone cannot establish why a difference occurred, whether it indicates an unacceptable risk, or what action is warranted.

BiasLens is built around a disciplined distinction between a **signal**, a **finding** and **proof**. Its approach makes room for uncertainty, evidence quality, context and human review.

### What we help organisations do
1. **Identify risk signals.** Examine AI-enabled processes and outcomes for patterns requiring investigation.
2. **Examine the evidence.** Record sources, assumptions, denominators, limitations and relevant context.
3. **Support accountable review.** Document risk judgements, human escalation, outstanding questions and recommended actions.
4. **Plan ongoing oversight.** Define how organisations can reassess changing systems and monitor emerging risks.

### Who we serve
- Risk, compliance and internal audit leaders
- AI governance and technology teams
- Boards and assurance committees
- Organisations procuring or deploying consequential AI systems

### Solutions
**AI Governance Readiness Assessment — available as a scoped professional service, subject to agreed terms.** A facilitated examination of one defined AI system or use case, its governance arrangements, evidence and risk priorities. Deliverables and exclusions are agreed before engagement.

**BiasLens Enterprise Assessment — platform-assisted offering under validation.** Structured evidence and bias-risk assessment with traceable review records. Availability depends on technical, security and accessibility acceptance testing.

**Continuous AI Assurance — proposed future offering.** Periodic assessment, Bias Drift monitoring and governance review. Do not market as a live automated capability until verified.

### Methodology
**A signal is not a finding. A finding is not proof.**

Our evidence-led approach distinguishes observations that merit investigation from conclusions supported by sufficient evidence. We account for provenance, context, uncertainty, privacy, small-group safeguards and human responsibility.

BiasLens assesses systems and aggregated processes, not individual worker productivity or employee worth.

### Inclusion and accessibility
Accessibility and disability inclusion are embedded in governance quality—not restricted to a separate niche offering. They form part of how relevant systems, user journeys and risks are evaluated.

### Trust and limitations
BiasLens supports governance assessment; it does not guarantee that an AI system is unbiased, legally compliant or free from discrimination. Mapping a methodology to a standard is not the same as certification or independent assurance.

Assessments rely on the scope and evidence available. Formal findings require contextual investigation and appropriately qualified review.
